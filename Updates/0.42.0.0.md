## Client
- Macros, in the `Macros` folder that appears after you log into a character.
  - There's a global macros file, and another for each character.
  - Macros can't be made in game yet. Exit to the conference and re-enter the world to reload them.

## Server
- Stun cancels fear, and includes the remaining round time.
- Tortuga, Asheron's pirate world, is open to explore through a portal near the Kesmai docks.
