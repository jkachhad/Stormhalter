## Client
- Light and lunar phase indicators on the spell panel.

## Content
- Fixed an issue where Sharks, Piranhas and Salamanders had no death sound.

## Server
- Visibility in dungeons no longer depends on the light cycle.
- Creature groups move with much better timing.
- Non-thieves are advised not to enter thief guild areas.
- A message tells you how far you fell.
- Shopkeepers confirm your purchase.
- Gold can be called `gold`, `coin` or `coins`.
- `!missing` on Discord creates an issue for a missing feature.
- Fixed an issue where summoned phantasms were removed in the wrong order.
- Fixed an issue where Naptha and Nitro didn't have their intended effects.
- Fixed an issue where poisons didn't stack or deal damage properly.
