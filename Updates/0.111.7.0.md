## Client
- Fixed an issue where your path went wrong in the client after moving.

## Server
- Mana now regenerates every 9 seconds (3 rounds), and every 12 seconds (4 rounds) for those who don't cast spells, such as Knights.
  - Lands of Kesmai used 6 seconds. Matching it would have made end-game spells and Knights' instant heals stronger still.
- Items and effects can carry three new stats.
  - Mana Recovery Delay changes your mana regeneration delay, from 3 to 15 seconds.
  - Spell Focus lowers the chance that damage interrupts a warmed spell, down to 25% (it was always 100% past a damage threshold).
  - Spell Clearcast is the chance that casting a warmed spell makes your next mana spell free, for up to 7 rounds.
- The adventurer bonus now runs on facet time. Before, it lasted 3 minutes across all facets.

## Razalas Peak
- Bosses give 50% fewer war points, and minions none.
- Warbands: a party leader joins the war with "The Arbiter, Join". A solo player gets a party of their own.
  - The party is locked until the war ends, and can't switch warbands.
  - The points needed grow with each member, and the champion has to reach a minimum and beat the other warbands.

## Collaborators
- New stats `EntityStat.ManaRecoveryDelay`, `EntityStat.SpellFocus` and `EntityStat.SpellClearcast`.
