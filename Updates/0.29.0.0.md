## Client
- You get a notification when switching between characters.
- The client knows more about pathing costs, so paths go around air and water hexes more easily.

## Server
- Creatures regenerate health only after combat ends.
- Your willpower decides whether fire and ice confuse you.
- Fixed an issue where `dump` locked your round.
- Fixed an issue where entering the world with a hidden status effect disconnected you.
- Fixed an issue where using stairs or ropes with Speed active delayed your round.
