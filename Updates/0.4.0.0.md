## Client
- The login screen shows the game version.

## Content
- Fixed an issue with the sound and body of summoned rats.

## Server
- New `swap` command moves the item in one hand to your free hand.
- New `wield` command moves an item from your belt to a free hand.
- New `belt` command moves an item from your hand to a free slot.
- Skills can be critiqued from any distance.
- Caster and ranged creatures keep their distance from an attacker.
- Jumpkicks no longer land after you suffer a sprain.
- Fixed an issue with caster creatures' melee attacks and combat messages.
- Fixed a crash with area of effect spells.

## Kesmai
- Kesmai -1 respawns faster, with more variety.
- The surface respawns faster, with more variety.

## Collaborators
- New sandbox command `heal` raises your health, stamina and mana to full.
