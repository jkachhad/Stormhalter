## Server
- Removed the tethering and home rules that bound creatures to specific areas or regions.
- Creatures from a region spawner wander within its bounds, unless dragged out.
- Tethered creatures from a location spawner wander the subregion they spawn in.
- Tethered creatures teleport back to where they spawned when out of combat.
- Tethered creatures may chase you up and down stairs.
- Vigor is now an instant spell that turns your stamina into charges.
- A friendly orc hasn't heard back from an expedition he sent out. Take some friends and investigate.
- The server restarts automatically at 10 PM PST.
