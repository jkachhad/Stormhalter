## Client
- `Esc` with an active target clears the target and any marked path.

## Content
- New icons for Shadowstep, Stalker in the Shadows, Perception, Backstab and Vigor.

## Server
- Channeled spells, starting with Vigor, an experimental Thief spell.
  - Vigor is available at Shadow Mage (13). It costs 8 mana to cast and 2 stamina a round to channel.
  - While it's channeled, attacking builds a stacking buff.
  - If your stamina runs out, the channel ends and the stack ticks down. Recast Vigor once your stamina regenerates to keep building it.
- Some spells need a direction when cast along a path, and can't start on your own hex.
  - Whirlwind, Poison Cloud, Create Portal, Concussion, Create Lava, Dragon Breath, Fire Bolt, Wall of Fire and Lightning Lance.
- Summoned frogs and rats leave the mortal plane after 20 minutes.
- `password` needs your current password (i.e `/password oldPassword newPassword`).
- Fixed an issue where forgiving a player wasn't kept at the next save.
- Added the missing `search` action to various walls.
- The Opal facet is open again.
- Fixed a disconnect when stepping into poisoned water.
- Fixed a crash when a player was dead during a restart.
- Fixed a crash when a player died on a teleporter.
