## WorldForge
- New `PoisonedWater` component. Its `Potency` is the starting poison damage.
- Fixed a crash when looking at `ItemTeleporter` properties.
- Fixed an issue where C# highlighting didn't show syntax errors.

## Server
- Open and close bottles in your hands (i.e `open bottle`, `close bottle`).
- Open bottles may spill when you run or move them.
- `drink` works on open bottles in your hands without breaking them.
- Creatures avoid walking on poisoned water.

## Collaborators
- Consumables have a `Content` property, and contents can move between consumables.
