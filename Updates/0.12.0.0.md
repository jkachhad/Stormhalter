## Client
- Your path on the world map is cleared only when the map shifts.

## Server
- Command aliases: `F` for `fight`, `D` for `down`, `U` for `up` and `C` for `cast`.
- You can type a path in command mode without `move` (i.e `E E E` for `move E E E`).
  - Invalid paths are ignored and have to be entered again (i.e `E E M`).
- Creatures you control take paths without `move` too (i.e `creature, E E S` for `creature, move E E S`).
  - Invalid paths are ignored and have to be given again (i.e `creature, E E M`).
- Commands such as `<target>, <order>` are turned into speech, so you can order creatures in command or speech mode.
- Normal doors never close on their own. Secret doors close after 1 minute.
- You can't leave the world while in a trance.
- The sheriff drove out the thief merchant and hired a powerful conjurer, who conjures all kinds of treasure in the lockers.
  - The sheriff also confiscated all thief bags.
- Fixed an issue where an invalid `climb down` locked your round forever.
- Fixed an issue where reef wasn't an obstruction.
- Fixed an issue where nocking a bow by command played no sound.
- Fixed an issue where players weren't properly removed from peeked creatures.
