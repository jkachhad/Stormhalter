## Client
- `Up` and `Down` put the text caret at the end of the input.
- Clicking the center tile of the map clears your path.
- Sound cues when pathing.
- Fixed an issue where the lockers didn't show stored items properly.
- Fixed an issue where the health bar didn't show if you entered the world with 100 health.
- Fixed an issue where the world updated twice for each move.

## Server
- New +1 and +2 dexterity rings. Rings give at most +2 dexterity.
- The conjurer has an experimental robe.
- All attacks give full skill except those that do little damage.
- Creatures attack nearby targets when their own target is out of range.
- Summoned phantasms float above water.
- Fixed an issue where miss messages didn't show after the earlier fix to the double block benefit.
- Fixed an issue where creatures kept attacking a target after it disconnected.
- Fixed an issue where the Griffin jacket couldn't be worn as a robe.
- Fixed an issue where you could portal through indestructible walls.

## Collaborators
- New sandbox commands.
  - `goname <name>` takes you to an entity matching the name (i.e `goname bear`, `goname sk`).
  - `invis` makes you invisible to everything around you.
  - `invul` makes you invulnerable to all damage.
  - `respawn` triggers a respawn on your segment.
