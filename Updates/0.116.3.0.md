## Server
- Hide in Shadows stays active next to a closed secret door.
- `search` now works on items on the floor (i.e `search corpse` rolls a corpse).
- You can now throw an item from your backpack, belt or hands.
  - `throw dagger from backpack at orc`, `throw axe from belt n nw`, `throw from sack` (the first throwable item in your sack), `throw dagger at orc`.
- You can now `take` items from and `drop` them into your locker (i.e `drop gems in locker`, `take robe from locker`).
- Only one player at a time can stand at a counter in town.
- Stacking items inside a container no longer uses a round.
- Banned accounts no longer show on the leaderboards.
- The server has a new address, `148.113.228.235`. It can take 24 to 48 hours, depending on your internet provider, for `play.stormhalter.com` to point to it.
- Fixed an issue where the Jeweler's Ring and Banker's Ring ignored your weight. If you're over-encumbered, the gold now drops to the floor.
- Fixed an issue where an item's spell proc or clearcast delayed your round.
- Fixed an issue where you could, rarely, be disconnected while dragging items.
- Fixed an issue where `Alia` crashed the server.
- Fixed an issue where a rope had the wrong climb-up action.
- Fixed an issue where a burning corpse restarted the fire damage timer, adding an extra tick of damage.
