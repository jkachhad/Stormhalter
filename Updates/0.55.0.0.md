## Client
- The numpad works for directions when Num Lock is off.
- Fixed an issue where terrain changes didn't draw properly with partial visibility.
- Spell icons show their mana cost and intensity.
- Hotkeys switch the secondary viewport (`Alt + C`, `Alt + R`, `Alt + S`, `Alt + B`, `Alt + L`, `Alt + P`).
- `Alt + H` toggles looking at the ground.
- Fixed an issue where replays didn't play properly after forwarding.
- Fixed an issue where sounds repeated when forwarding or rewinding replays.

## Server
- Sage takes commands in direct messages.
- Fixed an issue where items falling down stairs or shafts didn't update for those looking.
- Poisoned weapons turn lawful players neutral.
- Knights who don't stay lawful lose their knighthood.
- A lawful who kills a lawful turns neutral.
- Warming a spell interrupts mana regeneration.
- Visibility is calculated on a 9x9 grid instead of 7x7.
- Perception, an experimental Thief spell to perceive enemies near adjacent walls.
- Creatures can no longer walk into hidden doors.
