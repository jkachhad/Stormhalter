## Client
- Experience values are now shown with commas and shortened with a unit (i.e 1,000,000,000 shows as 1,000m; then b and t).

## Content
- New female Thief sprites.

## Server
- An account can now hold 8 characters, plus one for each year of the account's age.
- Characters can be moved between accounts, up to 3 plus one for each year of the account's age. Move the characters you care about most to your primary account.
- Secondary accounts can be linked to your primary account for now: only unauthenticated accounts can be linked, and only one of them can be logged in at a time.
- A website for your account, http://play.stormhalter.com, works with your Discord login.
  - Reset your password, see your reputation and account age, and transfer characters.
  - A status page shows who is connected.
- `Overkill`: an attack that kills with more than 5 times the enemy's maximum health gives no experience and no skill gain.
  - Spell damage can't cause `Overkill`.
- When your conjured creature kills an enemy that gives experience, you get 50% of it, split with your party as usual.
- You can upgrade up to 3 lockers, plus one for every 10 levels.
- All Knight quest NPCs now use interactions. If you're stuck, visit the earlier NPCs again.
- Items now decay later: each point of quantity adds 90 facet-minutes. Corpses still take 15 facet-minutes, and Poor and Common items 180.
