## Server
- Creatures take damage from their own spells again.
- Using an invalid conference room name moves you to the default room.
- Fixed a crash with Sven and various other invalid characters.

## Collaborators
- To stop a creature taking damage from its own spells, use `AddImmunity` or make it magic immune.
- Lightning doesn't stun a creature immune to `typeof(Lightning)`. It still takes damage unless it's also immune to `typeof(LightningBoltSpell)`.
