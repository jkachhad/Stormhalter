## Server
- Enchant Weapon works on more weapons: axes at 8, staves and flails at 11, and projectile weapons at 12.
- New `shoot` command, like `throw` but without a hotkey.
- `fight`, `jumpkick`, `throw` and `shoot` pick only hostile targets, including players who attack you.
- You can no longer shoot or throw items at yourself.
- Create Snake can no longer be cast on yourself.
- The Hummingbird Sword is now lawful.
- Blindness removes all creatures from your view again.
- Potion contents are kept through restarts.
- Pathing uses diagonal (octile) distance as its heuristic.
- Fixed a crash when saving hostility.
- Fixed an issue where spawners spawned extra creatures.

## Collaborators
- `AddWeakness` and `AddImmunity` accept spell types (i.e `entity.AddWeakness(typeof(WhirlwindSpell))`).
  - A magic-immune creature can still be weak to a particular spell.
