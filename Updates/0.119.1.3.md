## Client
- Chat tabs now have names, shown when the mouse rests on a tab.
  - Tabs you already have are named after what they show: `Speech`, `Combat`, `Party` or `Chat`. New tabs are `Tab 1`, `Tab 2` and so on.
  - A new character's `Combat` and `Party` tabs now have a sword and a goblet for icons, instead of the speech icon.
- Right-clicking a chat tab opens a menu: `Rename...`, `Font Size ▸`, `Chat Settings...`, `New Tab`, `Reset Position` and `Close Tab`.
  - `Chat Settings...` opens the chat settings at that tab, where the new `Name` box renames it too.
- The chat input now says where a line goes before you send it: `Command:`, `Say:` or `Party:`, in the line's color.
  - A leading `/` counts, so `/hello there` shows `Say:` while you're in command mode.
- After you send a message to your party, the input opens with `party ` already typed (`/party ` in speech mode), so your next line goes to the party too.
  - Delete it, send any other line, or switch between command and speech mode to stop.
- The chat input now remembers your last 32 lines, up from 10.
  - `Up` stops at the oldest line instead of going round to the newest, and `Down` past the newest brings back the line you were typing.
- You can now pick the colors of chat messages in the chat settings.
  - `Server`, `Party Speech` and each `Entity` channel have a swatch in the `Messages` list, and `Speaker names` has its own. Choose from 12 colors or `Default`.
  - Colors apply to every tab and change the lines already shown. System and combat messages keep the colors the server gives them.
- Fixed an issue where every chat line showed the same time after you changed a tab's font, size or timestamp format, or turned `Show Timestamp` off and on.
- Fixed an issue where messages that arrived in a tab you weren't looking at showed the time you opened the tab.
