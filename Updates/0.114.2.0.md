## Client
- Creatures and NPCs now have interactions.
  - Every one can be inspected; hostile ones can be attacked, jumpkicked and kicked, and `Pet` works on hostile players.
  - Magic trainers have "Sell: Book" and "Teach: <Spell>", and weapon trainers "Train: <Skill>".
