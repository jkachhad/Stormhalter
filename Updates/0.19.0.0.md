## Client
- Fixed an issue where sprites didn't load their default hue.
- Fixed an issue where alignments didn't show or update properly on the creature list.

## Server
- Thieves are no longer revealed when hit by Blind, Fear or Stun.
- Throwing a returning item from your belt moves it to your free hand.
- Creatures step on ice and fire if they're well protected.
- Salamanders cast spells even when enemies are next to them.
- Peacekeepers and vendors no longer loot gold without providing a service.
- You can log out 24 seconds after combat.
- Fixed an issue where some NPCs out of line of sight were visible after you moved.
- Fixed an issue where casting Venom and targeting it right away cleared the round.

## Kesmai
- Changed the elevations of Kesmai's regions.
