## Server
- `Inspect` and the trainers' `Critique skill ▸` and `Critique training ▸` now work when you aren't standing at the counter.
  - `Inspect` on an item another character is wearing now works from as far away as its menu opens.
- Dexterity rings now show their Dexterity on their tooltip (i.e `+2 Dexterity`).

## Collaborators
- Two stat changes. Read Stormhalter `Documentation/Kesmai.Server/StatModifiers.md`.
  - `EntityStat.MeleeAttackBonus`, `RangedAttackBonus` and `ProjectileAttackBonus` make attacks, throws and shots more likely to hit.
  - Dexterity rings give their Dexterity through the hidden `AgileStatus`, so don't also add `EntityStat.Dexterity` to a `DexterityRing`.
