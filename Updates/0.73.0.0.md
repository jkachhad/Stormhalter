## Client
- New read-only console for logging.
- Fixed an issue where pressing `Enter` didn't add your input to the up and down history.
- Fixed a crash when dragging stackable items such as gold.
- Fixed a crash with duplicate macro commands.

## Server
- Vendors and trainers answer to part of a name and to list numbers (i.e `oskar, teach close`, `oskar, teach 1`, `lars, sell weak`).
- Vendors won't serve you while you're hidden.
- `users` shows how many players are in your segment.
- An item that moves or changes amount is dropped from pending pick-ups (i.e another player grabs the Drake potion you're dragging).
- Fixed an issue where `take` locked your round when your hands were already full.
- Fixed an issue where a character's age reset to an invalid value.
- Fixed a hang around opening and open bottles.

## Axe Glacier
- Fixed some spawns and teleporters (Thanks Zrikz!).

## Bloodlands
- New spawns (Thanks Zrikz!).

## Collaborators
- `StockEntry` has an `AllowRestock` parameter to stop items restocking.
- `ItemEntity` has a `CanDisintegrate` property.
- `MobileEntity.Say` speaks a message that players in line of sight see (i.e `sherrif.Say("I'm the law around here.")`).
