## Server
- Balms stop on the tick you reach full health, not the next one.
- Enchant Weapon, as an experiment, adds an attack bonus to your wielded weapon for its next swing.
  - The enchantment is lost when you unwield the weapon or make a successful attack.
- All existing copper halberds were deleted.
