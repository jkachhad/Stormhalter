## Client
- Fixed an issue where dragging an item out of a locker slot could crash.
- Fixed an issue where ground item slots didn't reset their positions.

## Content
- New art for coins.

## Server
- Moving items to or from the locker no longer uses a round.
- Dying costs attributes, health and stamina again.
- Permadeath characters die for good only when the ghods resurrect them, so a Thaumaturge or a priest can now resurrect them.
  - The ghods resurrect you when your corpse burns or decays, or when you leave the world.
- Commands can pick their target with filters in `@[…]`, applied in order (i.e `cast at @[hostile:index(last)]`, `throw dagger at @[hostile:npc:far:casting]`, `jumpkick @[mouseover]`).
  - Who: `hostile`, `friendly`, `pc`, `npc` and `conjured`.
  - State: `injured`, `healthy`, `deathly`, `casting`, `melee`, `ranged`, `poisoned`, `feared`, `stunned` and `blind`.
  - Where and which: `near`, `distant`, `far`, `distance(n)`, `index(n|last)`, `serial(n)` and `mouseover`.
  - `fight` and `kick` always add `near:hostile`, `jumpkick` adds `distant:hostile`, and `shoot` and `throw` add `hostile`.
