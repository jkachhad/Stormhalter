## Server
- You can now whisper to players on your tile; players elsewhere only see that you're whispering (i.e `/whisper I speak to you in confidence`).
- Creatures can now throw weapons (i.e hammers).
- The Thaumaturge's Summon Snake now scales with player level. (Thanks Brick!)
- A permadeath character can now be resurrected at most three times, once from each source: the Thaumaturge's Raise Dead, an item that resurrects, and a priest.
  - Your character sheet shows which sources you've used. Once you've used all three, or the ghods resurrect you, follow https://tinyurl.com/helpidied
- Blackburrow and Bloodlands are now open to permadeath characters.
- A dead permadeath character can only enter the world within 10 minutes of its death, and a deleted character can't enter it at all.
- Fixed an issue where permadeath characters couldn't be deleted.
- Fixed an issue where other players couldn't hear your speech.
- Fixed an issue where changing your maximum health didn't update the health shown.
- Fixed several spawning issues.
  - A full spawner no longer creates extra monsters, and its timer starts only once it has room again.
  - Spawners fill entries below their minimum first, and retry a failed spawn later instead of stalling.

## Collaborators
- Equipped items (paperdoll, rings, hands) can listen to speech and whispers: set `CanListen = true` and override `OnHearSpeech` and `OnHearWhisper`.
