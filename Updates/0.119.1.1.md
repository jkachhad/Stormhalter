## Server
- Escorts, and pets you tell to follow you, now keep following around corners, through doors, and up and down stairs, ropes and shafts.
  - When they lose sight of you, they go to where they last saw you.
- Escorts now keep following you after a fight. Before, they stood still until you chose `Follow` in their menu again.
- Escorts now tell you when they can't find you and stop to wait, when they stop to fight, and when they follow you again.
  - When you recruit one, it explains that `Follow me` keeps it on your tile and `Trail me` a step behind you.
- Another player can now take an escort whose player has logged out or hasn't been near it for 5 minutes.
- Fixed an issue where escorts on `Trail me` fell behind and stopped following.
  - The slowest escorts, which never moved on `Trail me`, now walk a tile a round.
  - An escort that was wandering when you chose `Trail me` now follows you instead.
