## Client
- Chat windows scroll with the mouse wheel.

## Server
- `eat` works like `drink`. All Mugwort, Red Berries and Yellow Berries in the world were deleted.
- Creatures and players stay in combat for 12 rounds after dealing or taking damage.
- Regeneration is simpler, and moving no longer interrupts it.
  - Health regenerates in full every round out of combat and at 25% in combat, and stops when you deal or take damage.
  - Stamina regenerates every 2 rounds once health is full, unless you're encumbered. Mana regenerates every 3 rounds unless you're casting or channeling.

## Collaborators
- `Mugwort`, `RedBerries` and `YellowBerries` now inherit from the new `Food` class.
