## Client
- The replay window has an exit button that takes you back to the login window.
- Fixed an issue where container panels didn't reset when rewinding a replay.

## Server
- Stacking items always uses a round.
- With `cast`, Venom picks your right hand first, then your left, then the target.
- Jumpkick takes encumbrance into account.
- You can interact only with items on your own location.
- The spell sequence resets only after all fizzle checks are done.
- Caster creatures finish their cast after their target moves into melee range.
- Fixed a crash when stacking items.
