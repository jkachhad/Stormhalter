## Client
- The character sheet shows encumbrance.

## Server
- Spells can strike critically for bonus damage.
- Combat logs for ranged and direct spell attacks.
- `kick` deals bashing damage using your hand skill.
- New `password` command changes your password in the conference room.
- Snakes from Create Snake count as summoned, and match their caster's alignment.
- Searching a wall dispels Create Portal.
- Gold's description shows its amount.
- The server restarts at 2 AM PST (5 AM EST).

## Leng
- Shidosha now drops the fan.

## Collaborators
- New `CreatureEntity.IsTethered` property.
  - Tethered creatures teleport to their `Home` if they move far away, and don't leave their region to chase combatants up or down stairs.
  - Untethered creatures move freely in their segment, but avoid wandering into a lair or town.
