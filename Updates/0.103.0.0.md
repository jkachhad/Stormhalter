## Client
- Network logging is off during replays, so seeking is faster.
- The information bar is back when watching replays.
- Console logging settings are saved.

## WorldForge
- WorldForge warns when a region's ID is 0.

## Server
- A creature's combat timer restarts whenever it swings, or warms or casts a spell. Evil creatures start regenerating 10 rounds later, chaotic ones 5.
- `take` moves items to your hands and `sack` to your backpack (i.e `take <item> from <location>`, `take <#> ring off <left/right>`, `sack <item>`).
- `take gold` stacks with gold already in your hands or inventory.
- Carrying several corpses moves all their ghosts.
- All creatures recalculate their vision when the light changes.
- Fixed a small chance of a server and client crash when recalling on stairs.

## Collaborators
- `CreatureEntity.OnSpellCast(Spell)` is called when a spell starts (targeting, casting), and `OnSpellCastFinished(Spell)` when it finishes.
