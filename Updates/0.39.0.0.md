## Client
- The `OK` button in the Locate window can be pressed by focusing it (`Tab`, then `Enter`).

## Server
- Fixed an issue where some stuns lasted rounds instead of seconds.
- Added various missing robes.
- Added the missing shields.
- Dragons cast Dragon's Breath further ahead to reach distant targets.
- The Knight's Cure spell can only be cast on targets in your hex.
- Fixed pathing for various Wizard and Thaumaturge spells.
  - Spells can't path through water or closed doors, or over walls and obstructions.
  - The longest path through a target is 10, when skill level doesn't set it.
  - Spells can only be cast within the 7x7 world view.
- Lightning Storm and Fire Storm follow the same rules, and only spread to tiles in line of sight.
- Lightning Bolt follows the same rules, and lands on your own hex when cast from water.
- Lightning Lance and Lightning Storm follow rules like Lightning Bolt's.
- Concussion reaches another 10 feet.
- Fixed an issue where Concussion's center tile didn't have the right graphic.
