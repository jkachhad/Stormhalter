## Server
- All items with charges show them.
- Weapons give skill based on their swing delay.
- Characters that log in with invalid values have them reset (i.e a Thief with 1000 mana goes back to the most his level allows).

## Collaborators
- New `SpawnLocation` property sets where tethered creatures teleport to.
- `Home` strictly limits where a creature may path.
