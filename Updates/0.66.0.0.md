## Server
- `again` (or `a`) repeats your previous command.
- `push <item> [<index>] <direction|up|down>` moves an item, and you with it, in the given directions (i.e `push bottle 2 up`).
- Players can be marked as aggressors with defensive flags, shown by a purple name.
  - Aggressions last through restarts and expire after a few days.
- `pet` clears aggressions from another player.
- `report` creates a GitHub issue from the client.
- `drop` works like `dump`, but on what's in your hands.
  - `drop` drops a single item from your hands, and `drop dagger` a single dagger.
  - `drop daggers` drops every item matching the singular form, and `drop all` everything in your hands.
- Followers carry out `take`, `dump` and `drop` orders the way players do (i.e `ph, take gold from counter`, `ph, dump gems on counter`, `ph, dump all`, `ph, drop gold`).
- Fixed an issue where followers didn't pick up or drop items on counters properly.
- `take` and `sack` fail if you're already picking up an item.
- Fixed an issue where a recent change broke swing delays on Opal and Pearl.
- `break` gives experience only for items that count as treasure.
- Wall stuns depend on the path you asked for and how far you moved.
- Harming yourself no longer shows a defensive flag message.
- Fixed an issue where spawns from map projects didn't cast spells.
- Breaking another player's items turns a lawful player neutral.
- Ropes and shafts have a chance to make you fall.
- `climb down` works on shafts.
- Undid a change that lowered monsters' minimum damage by 2.
- Fixed an issue where spamming movement reset the round timer.
- Two new facets: Topaz, with 2.5 second rounds, and Ruby, with 1.5 second rounds.
  - Rounds and experience and skill rates: Agate and Beryl 3.0s at 2.0x, Topaz 2.5s at 2.5x, Pearl 2.0s at 3.0x, Ruby 1.5s at 3.5x, Opal 1.0s at 4.0x.

## Collaborators
