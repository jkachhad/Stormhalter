## Client
- The world window matches the resolution in your settings.
- You can bring up an instant spell's target between rounds.

## Server
- Parties, with the `party` command.
  - `/party create <name>` creates a party, and `/party join <name>` joins one; its leader must be in range.
  - `/party leave` leaves your party (a new leader takes over if the leader leaves), and `/party disband` disbands it.
  - `/party <message>` talks to your party.
- Party members in range share experience, each getting their level divided by the party's total levels.
- You earn party experience if you're near the attacker or the creature.
- Party members can attack each other without flagging.
- `roll` rolls a d20 and shows the result to players in line of sight.
- You can't exit right after dying.
- Shadowstep can't be cast on your own hex.
- Shadowstep can be cast on staircases.
- Fixed an issue where stun protection wasn't calculated and applied properly for melee hits.
