## Server
- Fixed an issue where darkness was left out of some visibility checks, so Lightning Storm, Fire Storm, Dragon Breath, Fireball and Ice Storm now cast through and onto darkness.
- Items thrown onto sky hexes now fall through.
- Teleporting onto a tile now triggers it (teleport, fire, sky and so on).
- Shadowstep can be cast onto any teleport tile, such as sky hexes and portals.
