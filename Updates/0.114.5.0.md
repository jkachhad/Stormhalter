## Content
- Two experimental female Thief sprites, bodies 123 (120 pixels) and 124 (240 pixels).
- Fixed an issue where the female Knight sprites had extra pixels and two female Martial Artist sprites were misaligned.

## Server
- Authentication now binds one account to one Discord account, and has been reset: authenticate your main account again.
  - A bound account can't change its Discord account later, and authentication has to be renewed every 30 days.
- Reputation ranks now come from your account's age and play time: (account age / 2) + (play time × 2).
  - The ranks are Strider (6), Wanderer (3), Drifter (1) and Newcomer (0).
  - Your Discord rank updates each night and when you authenticate.
- Each calendar month, at a nightly restart, the Arbiter looks for a new high warlord, who gets a special rank on Discord.
  - Fixed an issue where the warlord was announced several times after a reset.
- `party list` shows your party, and `party kick <name>` removes someone until the party is locked into a warband.
- Fixed an issue where several characters from the same account couldn't join a party.
