## Client
- The major log scrolls to the newest message when its scroll bar is at the bottom.
- Switched to an updated version of the Lidgren networking library.
- Fixed an issue where message logs sometimes didn't scroll automatically.

## Content
- New art for some of the new weapons.

## Server
- Frogs from Wizard's Eye match the caster's alignment.
- Creatures pick a new target when they can't search for their previous one.
- Create Lava flows in a straight line for a time.
  - It falls from heights to lower levels, and drops items and creatures properly.
  - A flow indicator shows, for debugging.
- Spells cast through items no longer interrupt mana regeneration.
- Combat logs for jumpkick and throw attacks.
- Combat logs show the extra damage of critical strikes.

## Collaborators
- A creature's chance to cast comes from `MeleeSpellChance`, with its combatant in melee range, and `RangeSpellChance`, out of melee range.
  - Set them when constructing `CreatureSpellCollection(double meleeCastChance, double rangeCastChance)`.
  - `RangeSpellChance` defaults to `25.0` instead of `100.0`.
  - `CreatureSpellCollection(meleeCastChance, rangeCastChance)` defaults to `meleeCastChance`.
