## Client
- Items now show a tooltip when you hover them.
  - It lists the item's stats, bonuses and effects, with its price and weight.
  - Hold `Shift` to see what the badges beside it mean.
  - `Item Tooltips` in the settings turns it off and brings back the name label.
- Shift+Right Click menus have a new look.

## Server
- Unidentified items now hide their stats until they are identified.
  - Items bought from a merchant, coins and plain swords (i.e a longsword) never need identifying.
  - New characters start with identified gear. Gear you already have stays as it is.
- An item's level requirement now shows on its tooltip, in red until you reach it.
- Fixed an issue where a server restart other than the nightly one could lose recent progress.

## Staff
- `props` is now a game master command.
- Staff can identify an item with `Identify` in its Shift+Right Click menu.

## Collaborators
- Items now fill in their own tooltips. Read `Documentation/Kesmai.Server/ItemTooltips.md`.
  - `GetTooltipDescription` gives the item text. `GetTooltipEntries` adds lines with `AddEffect` and `AddLevelProgress`.
  - `GetTooltipEntries` and `GetClientProperties` take a `TooltipContext`: `Wearer` holds the item, `Observer` looks at it.
  - Items need identifying unless `RequiresIdentification` is `false`. Put level checks in `GetRequiredLevel`.
