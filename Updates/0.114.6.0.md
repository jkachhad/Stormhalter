## Server
- Reputation ranks now depend only on your total play time.
- The server does less work each round.
  - Creature groups keep their timers in their own region, so rescheduling is 60% faster and only that region is affected when it wakes or sleeps.
  - Line of sight is worked out 10% to 40% faster, and up to 40 times faster for a creature with full sight, which also cut 40 seconds off server start.
