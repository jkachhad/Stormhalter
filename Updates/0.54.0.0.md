## Client
- `w` is no longer an alias of `warm`.
  - If it still warms, update your global macros.

## Server
- Fixed an issue where entities didn't update their visibility after terrain changes (i.e darkness, doors closing).
- Ranged and caster creatures move away from you, but not so far that they lose line of sight.
- Ranged and caster creatures try to find a safe place when drowning.
- Stun messages repeat only for the stunned one.
- Casting Blind, Fear or Stun counts as an aggressive action.
- Summoned Djinns and Efreets are immune to their own spell types.
- Commands can name a target by its number on the creature list (i.e `throw dagger at hobgoblin1`, or `throw dagger at hob12` for the 12th hobgoblin).
- The server restarts every morning at 4 AM.
