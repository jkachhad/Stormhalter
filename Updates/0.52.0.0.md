## WorldForge
- Some windows can be moved freely.
- Fixed an issue with changing or moving selections.

## Server
- Track your training with `trainer, critique <skill> training`.
- Hold `Alt` to cast Venom automatically; otherwise a target appears.
  - Venom uses your right hand first, then your left hand, belted daggers, then any belted melee weapon.
- `sack` makes picking up items easier (i.e `sack gem`, `sack gem from counter`).
- Shadowstep teleports you to the pathed hex, through destructible walls but not obstructions.
  - Where you land, nearby creatures attack worse (more misses, less damage). It applies once and doesn't refresh.
  - The hex you land on must have a floor.
- Concussion is less likely to destroy walls, and more likely with skill level.
- Flying creatures ignore ground movement costs.
- Create Lava sweeps players, creatures and items along its flow and damages them.

## Oakvael
- Added the missing Knight trainer in Oakvael.
