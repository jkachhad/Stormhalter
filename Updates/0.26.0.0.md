## Client
- The character sheet shows a defense sources calculation, for testing.
- Fixed a crash when watching replays.
- Fixed an issue where items couldn't be equipped from the world.

## Server
- New experimental Thief spell at level 2: Hoodwink (8 mana).
  - It turns gold in your right hand into worthless gemstones that creatures can't tell from a real gemstone.
- Knight spells check that you're wearing a bound ring.
- Knights holding a hummer in their right hand change body.
- Going from Untrained to Awkward takes fewer swings than going from Awkward to Mediocre.
- Darkness can't be placed over fire.
- A spell clears your target only if the target came from that spell.
- Fixed an issue where `The spell fails` showed while targeting Knight spells.
- Fixed an issue where casting Turn Undead disconnected you.
- Fixed an issue where a Whirlwind placed on an invalid tile disconnected you.
- Fixed an issue where invalid tree terrain disconnected you.

## Leng
- A treasure conjurer works in the Leng lockers (`goname treasure`).

## Collaborators
