## Client
- Double-clicking a container slot works outside the item sprite.
- Right-clicking with an active target cancels the target.
- Right-clicking cancels your path before it cancels a target.
- Additional sprites are drawn under the player.

## Content
- Death sounds play a moment after the fatal blow.

## Server
- Reworked Poison Cloud, so it blinds and applies poison ticks properly.
- New `nock` and `unnock` commands for projectile weapons.
- Robes give mana regeneration.
- New `release` order for followers, like `begone`.
- Followers also respond to `#, (command)` orders.
- Followers are dispelled when you reach the limit.
- Gems are treasure, so Disintegrate no longer removes them.
- Trainers tell you when you're below your potential skill.
- Trainers train from your achieved skill, not your current skill.
- Moving items doesn't always use a round, especially within the same container.
- Moving items between your hands and the world always uses a round.
- Fire burns corpses to a cinder.
- Creatures move onto hexes with portal effects.
- Changed the intensity and range of Concussion.
- Caster creatures melee their target when they don't have enough mana.
- Secret doors leave the right terrain when destroyed.
- Fixed an issue where floors weren't generated under destructible walls.
- Fixed an issue where status effects from equipment didn't refresh after a server restart.
- Fixed an issue where you could summon followers past the limit after logging back in.
- Fixed an issue where Summon Phantasm's spell intensity reset.
- Fixed an issue where throwable items cleared the round, allowing instant throws.

## Kesmai
- Kesmai -1 holds up to 60 creatures (was 48).
