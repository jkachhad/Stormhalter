## Client
- Item tooltips can now open at a fixed place on the screen.
  - `Tooltip Position` on the `UI` tab of the settings chooses `Mouse`, as before, or one of nine places, such as `Top Right` or `Center`.
- Fixed an issue where an item tooltip near the edge of the screen could be partly cut off.
