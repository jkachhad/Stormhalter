## Client
- The item tooltip settings on the `UI` tab are now together on the `Item Tooltips` line.
  - `Position` sits beside the checkbox and is greyed out while tooltips are off.
  - The new `Delay` sets how long the mouse has to rest on an item before its tooltip opens, from 0 to 1000 ms. It starts at 0, as before.
- When the tooltip opens at a fixed place, the item under the mouse shows its name again.

## Server
- The Knight and Fighter class rings now make your attacks, throws and shots more likely to hit at ring levels 5 and 6 (i.e `+2 Melee Attack Bonus`).
  - Before, they gave Dexterity and took the same amount off your dodge.
- Fixed an issue where absorbing a Dexterity Ring into a class ring didn't raise your Dexterity until you put the class ring on again.

## Collaborators
- `EntityStat.Dodge` gives dodge from items and statuses. Read Stormhalter `Documentation/Kesmai.Server/StatModifiers.md`.
  - Item tooltips now show `Dodge` and the attack bonus stats from v0.119.0.3.
  - `StatModifierSet.BaseDodge` still works but never shows on a tooltip, so move items to `Add(EntityStat.Dodge, …)`.
