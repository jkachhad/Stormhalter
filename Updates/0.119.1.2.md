## Client
- The creature list now rearranges much less when many creatures are in view.
  - Creatures are grouped by how many steps away they are, as range is counted, and keep their places within a group.
  - While the mouse is over the list or you're choosing a target, it holds still: new creatures go at the end, and ones that leave stay dimmed.
- A scrolled creature list now stays on the same creature when the list rearranges.
- The new `Creature List` line on the `UI` tab sets the list's `Order`: `Nearest first`, `In order seen`, `Furthest first` or `Most injured`.
  - `Most injured` puts the most hurt creatures first, by the health shown on each row.
- Fixed an issue where double-clicking a creature in the list could attack another creature when the list rearranged between the clicks.

## WorldForge
- Escorts made with the Quest Maker can now be recruited with `Follow me` or `Trail me` in their menu, by the player on the quest.
