## Content
- New art for gold coins.

## Server
- Hit, miss and block rolls use a d1000 instead of a d20.
  - Any skill increase helps right away, rather than every level or every 4 levels.
- Balms no longer stop once they've restored your maximum health. They keep ticking, from 14 health down to 1.
- New experimental spell: Make Recall Ring (2), at magic level 15.
- Every member of a creature group plunders loot, not just the leader.
- Creatures move on once they've pillaged all they can carry.
- Anguished spirits keep you from travelling between segments.
- Thief portals let only thieves' corpses be carried through.
- Corpses can't be thrown at creatures or places.
- Fixed an issue where ranged creatures moved further than allowed when backing away from an attacker.
- Fixed an issue where `dump` said it wasn't a valid command, though it worked.
- Fixed an issue where you couldn't look at segment portals for treasure.
- Fixed an issue where travelling to another segment didn't reset recall rings.
