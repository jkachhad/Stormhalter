## Client
- Hotkeys are here, a major change to how input works.
  - `Enter` opens the input line, and `Enter` again submits it.
  - Hotkeys are set per character, like macros, and load when you enter the world.
- Changed the render scale of the Login and Settings windows.
- Fixed a crash when opening the Locate spell window.
- Fixed an issue where replays always stopped after hitting the exit button.

## Server
- `drink` drinks straight from your hand or backpack.
- `jumpkick` and `kick` pick their targets the way `fight` does.
- Items looted by creatures stack properly, so rolling their corpses no longer leaves empty spaces.
- Corpses can't be put in your locker.
