## Client
- The client checks its version and tells you when there's an update.
- Changed the encryption library's parameters.
- You can lift an item between actions to get ready to drop it.
- You can look at the ground between actions.

## Content
- A longer delay between a swing's sound and the damage cue.
- Fixed an issue where fizzle sounds didn't play properly in the spell sequence.
- Fixed an issue where the wrong sounds played.

## Server
- Some tiles are naturally covered in darkness until it's dispelled.
- Ruins interrupt movement.
- Skeletons no longer leave corpses.
- Fixed an issue where trainers didn't recognize attempts to train the hand skill.
- Fixed an issue where trainers didn't critique your progress properly.
- Fixed an issue where spellbooks weren't bound at the start.
- Fixed an issue where failing a spell didn't use mana properly.
- Fixed an issue where attacking an invalid target locked your round.

## Kesmai
- Wyverns on Kesmai -1 no longer flee.
- Fixed an issue where Lars and Sven didn't recognize their service counters.

## Collaborators
- New sandbox commands `setskill <skillName> <0-19>`, `go <x> <y> <regionId>` and `where`.
