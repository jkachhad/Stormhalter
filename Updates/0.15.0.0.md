## Client
- Rewrote drag and drop for items. Only the world panel needs the pointer over the item sprite.
- Holding `Enter` repeats it.
- Held keys repeat every 50 ms (was 100 ms).
- You start in command mode when you enter the game.
- Fixed an issue where the spell status panel didn't show its scroll arrows.

## Server
- Attributes have inborn minimums and maximums, and a live maximum.
  - You'll have to remake your characters to get the fixed limits.
- When the ghods take strength as the sacrifice for resurrection, maximum strength above 17 is taken too.
- You no longer need a free hand to `swap` the items in your hands.
- Creature AI searches corpses and loots treasure as it wanders.
- Flying creatures fly silently over water.
- The Black Rapier and the Hummingbird sword are in for testing.
- Weapons can give mana regeneration when held in the right hand (i.e Stormhalter).
- Whirlwind dispels fire and Poison Cloud.
- Fire placed on the map works like fire from spells.
- Darkness that can't be dispelled works like a floor, so you can path through it.
- Corner walls and pillars next to indestructible walls are indestructible too.
- Fixed an issue where items with weapon attributes didn't use their flags (i.e blue glow).
- Fixed an issue where unusual weapons, such as gauntlets, weren't treated as weapons.
- Fixed several line of sight issues with darkness and corpses, which could show creatures with gray health.
- Fixed an issue where some altars weren't tagged properly.
- Fixed a crash with spells and tiles that don't exist.
