## Client
- Fixed an issue where a single click sent an invalid cancel for lifting an item.
- Fixed an issue where teleporting creatures stayed on the creature list.

## Server
- Fixed an issue where shields and weapons didn't apply their base armor bonus against non-weapon attacks.
- Gauntlets use their projectile protection against projectiles, instead of their base armor bonus.
- Any item you're dragging is put back when you disconnect.
- Pathing now costs 255 through impassable terrain and 128 through sky.
- The copper halberd binds, is lawful and glows blue.
- Creatures follow you up and down stairs even when the stairs lead to the same region.
- Venom is now a direct damage poison.
- Each poison tick rolls for direct damage, based on direct versus total potency.
- Items lose a charge when stripped from corpses if they were equipped on the paperdoll or rings.
- Concussion is more likely to destroy walls (5% at magic level 8, 33% at 19).
- Orders to NPCs no longer show in the message log.
- Reworked Turn Undead.
- Creatures loot only hexes they can see.
- Creatures loot only while carrying fewer than 5 items.
- Darkness now obscures Perception, but you keep line of sight.
- Names can contain `.` until there's a guild system.
- Hoodwink gems crumble when creatures lift them.
- Shadowstep is an instant spell, but still uses a round.
- After a restart, regions go inactive after 50 rounds.
- Only breaking other players' items turns you neutral.
- Backstab attacks with both the left and right daggers.
- Spawn times (minimum and maximum) scale with the facet delay.
- All salamanders are now animals, and don't loot.

## Blackburrow
- Fixed the entrance to Blackburrow.

## Collaborators
- Poisons have an `IsDirect` property.
