## Client
- The client supports colored and alpha-blended terrain sent by the server.
- Fixed an issue where an item dropped during an inactive round disappeared from view.

## Server
- Weapons describe your proficiency in their skill.
- Sky drops tell you the distance.
- Creatures search only players' corpses.
- Creatures loot only items that can't bind.
- Items in containers are deleted along with their parent only when the parent is a player.
- Fixed an issue where shafts were converted as stairs.
  - You can't climb down a shaft, and it's one-way unless you find a rope below.
- Fixed an issue where creature AI looped when it couldn't find its opponent.
- Fixed an issue where blocks counted twice for some creatures (i.e wyverns).

## Collaborators
- New sandbox command `ShowSkills` shows your skills with their point values, to confirm you're gaining skill.
